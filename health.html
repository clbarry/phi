Personal Health Informatics (PHI) PhD Qualifying Exam

Paper Requirement: Health Component

The PHI Qualifying Exam consists of three components: Research Core, Health Component, and Technology Component. Each component will be assessed through a written paper designed to evaluate the candidate's knowledge and skills in the respective area. The goal of this exam is to assess a student’s aptitude for research, and to expose the student to new ideas and to faculty members who may broaden thinking about research topics in personal health informatics.
